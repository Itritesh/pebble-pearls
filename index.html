The Baroque Difference

Unlike perfectly spherical pearls, baroque pearls possess an organic, irregular contour. This defining trait is a testament to natural creation—ensuring no two pearls, and no two pieces of our jewelry, are ever exactly alike.

We hand-select these unique gems for their remarkable luster, iridescence, and captivating shapes, setting them in recycled 14k gold to create wearable storytelling.
